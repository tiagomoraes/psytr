# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People in regular therapy who already use file-capable AI agents (Hermes, Claude Code, OpenCode, OpenClaw, Cowork) as daily operators — and want their psychological self-observations documented in a sound structure between sessions. They value privacy and plain text; they don't want another app to open. Typical moment of use: right after a difficult situation ("preciso falar do que aconteceu"), or right before a session ("gera o report da semana"). They are not looking for an AI therapist: they already have a therapist.

## Product Purpose

sintr is a private mood diary you keep with your AI assistant: an agent-native psychological ledger made of a portable skill plus Markdown templates that teach a file-capable agent to conduct a supportive capture conversation, structure entries the way CBT thought records do, log check-ins, keep therapy context, and produce a weekly report the person reviews and sends to their therapist. There is no app, dashboard, database, or account. The agent is the interface; the files are the product. Success is measured in weeks captured and reports that reach sessions — not time-on-app.

## Brand Personality

Calm, private, precise. Like a well-kept notebook a trusted professional would recognize — not a wellness app performing empathy. No streaks, no scores-as-judgment, no "let's turn that frown upside down". Exact words over polished summaries; observation over interpretation. Three words: **calm, precise, discreet.**

## Anti-references

- "AI therapist" or "AI psychologist" claims — sintr documents; professionals treat.
- Wellness-app gamification: streaks, badges, guilt mechanics, confetti.
- Diagnosis-by-LLM: labeling ("you're anxious"), scoring moods as good/bad.
- Generic AI-startup gloss: gradients, glassmorphism, purple-blue, emoji, hype copy.
- Clinical software aesthetics: medical dashboards, red alerts, charts of "symptom severity" for a personal tool.

## Design Principles

1. **The person's words are sacred.** Verbatim thoughts; their emotion vocabulary; their language.
2. **Capture first, analyze later.** Never gate recording behind completeness or quality.
3. **Nothing leaves without consent.** Sharing is explicit, per instance, and recorded.
4. **Structure serves the session.** The schema exists to make therapy sessions sharper, not to grade the week.
5. **Calm by default.** Quiet weeks are fine; empty fields are fine; the tool never nags.

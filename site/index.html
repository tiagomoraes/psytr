Get started

The week between sessions, kept in your own words.

sintr is a private mood diary you keep by talking to your AI assistant. Before each therapy session, your week is waiting on one page, ready for you and your therapist.

Get started See how it works

Therapy gets fifty minutes a week. Your week happens in the rest of it.

By the session, Tuesday is a blur. You remember how you feel today, and what shook you midweek never quite gets told.

Tuesday, 7:40 pm. Tell it while it's fresh.

You tell your assistant what happened, the way you'd tell a friend. It asks one or two questions, then saves it.

On ordinary days, one line is enough.

Can we write down what happened at planning? I'm still wound up.

Of course. What went through your mind right then, and how strong was the anxiety, from 0 to 100?

“There's no way I can handle this.” About 85.

Saved. We can look at that thought more closely now, or leave it for your session.

Thursday, 8:50 am

Mood 2 of 5, slept 5½ hours.

Noted for Thursday.

Sunday, 8 pm. The week comes together.

Your assistant turns it into a short PDF: how the week went, what you want to talk about, a chart of your mood and sleep, and the moments that stood out, in your own words. Patterns are noted as observations, never as diagnoses.

Your thoughts stay yours.

On your computer
A folder, like a notebook in a drawer. No app, no account, no cloud.
Locked to you
Other people using the same computer can't open it.
Never passed around
Nothing is copied into chats or other services, and deleting means deleting.

One thing to know: the AI assistant you talk to usually sends what you type to the company behind it. Choose one you're comfortable with. Technical details

Start in three steps.

You need an AI assistant that runs on your computer and can save files there, like Claude Code, Hermes, OpenCode or OpenClaw. Browser-only chats can't. Claude Cowork needs one extra manual step, described on the technical page.

  1. Copy the setup message

    It's written for your assistant: where to get sintr and how to set it up.

    Copy setup message

  2. Paste it to your assistant

    It sets everything up and explains each step. It may ask permission first; that's normal.

  3. Answer a few questions

    Your language, time zone, therapist and session day. Then just talk to it when you need to.

Read the setup message before you copy it
Please set up sintr for me. It's a private journal for therapy that you'll keep for me as files on this computer. Clone https://github.com/tiagomoraes/sintr.git, read docs/INSTALL.md, and run the installer that matches you (./installers/install.sh with hermes, claude-code or generic), using ~/sintr as my journal folder. Explain each step to me in plain words before you do it. Then open ~/sintr/config.md and fill it in with me: ask for my language, time zone and therapy details one at a time, and leave empty anything I don't give you. Keep require_approval: true, and never send anything to anyone without asking me first.

It only uses what's published in the sintr project.

sintr keeps the record. Your therapist does the therapy.

It isn't therapy, a diagnosis or an emergency service. It won't label you or tell you what to do.

Scope: the sintr home page `site/index.html` at sintr.tiagomoraes.cloud. Mode: Persuade. Sibling surface: `site/tech/index.html` (Read), which holds every technical detail.

Audience: a person in regular therapy who is not technical. Job: understand why documenting the week matters, feel that it is safe, and start in three steps. Proof on hand: the fictional week (capture, check-in, the Portuguese example report with EN toggle, session notes), shown as notebook cards with human labels. Constraints: white ground, black CTAs, window light as the only color, no-JS and reduced-motion safe, English copy. On this page: no jargon (skill, ledger, Markdown, installer, repo, plugin, cron) and no raw code, except the setup message for the assistant, which is labeled as such.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the white wall of a quiet consulting room; window light crosses it as the week between two sessions passes. It speaks to the person, not the tool. It refuses both the wellness-app default (pastel blob hero, phone mockup, feature-card trio) and the developer-tool default (terminal mocks, install commands up front).

OWN-WORLD: White plaster ground, graphite ink, black pill buttons. The window light is the only color: a skewed 2×3 window of soft gradient (sky, peach, lavender, sage) with blurred sage leaf shadows, changing with the time of day. Editorial light serif display (Source Serif 4) and a quiet grotesk body (Hanken Grotesk). Artifacts are notebook cards: white paper, sky-tinted ruled hairlines, a peach margin rule, human labels in the sans, the person's words in the serif, quoted thoughts in the peach highlighter. Type size alone carries the hierarchy: no icon tiles, no feature cards, no eyebrows.

STORY: In the hero the visitor learns what sintr is: a private mood diary kept by talking to their AI assistant, with the week waiting on one page before each session. The next section gives the why: therapy gets fifty minutes, and life happens in the rest of the week. Then come two scenes: Tuesday's conversation, with a one-line Thursday check-in inside it, and Sunday's report, which needs your yes before it goes anywhere, closing on Monday. After that: privacy in three points, and three steps to start. Each idea appears exactly once.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav: serif wordmark on the left; How it works, Privacy, Under the hood, and a black "Get started" pill. Left seven columns: a three-line serif headline at about 5.6rem with "your own words." highlighted; a two-sentence lede that calls sintr a private mood diary; a black "Get started" button and a "See how it works" link. Right: window light with swaying leaf shadows, and the one notebook card on the page resting low in the light, writing itself on load, with a verbatim quoted thought. The upper-right wall is held empty.
